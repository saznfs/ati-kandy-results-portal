📖 Official SLIATE Academic Grading System

5.10 Grading System

Marks obtained in respect of a course unit are graded according to a twelve-category system shown as follows:

Range of Marks Grade Grade Points
85 – 100A+4.00
70 – 84A4.00
65 – 69A-3.70
60 – 64B+3.30
55 – 59B3.00
50 – 54B-2.70
45 – 49C+2.30
40 – 44C2.00 (Minimum Pass)
35 – 39C-1.70
30 – 34D+1.30
25 – 29D1.00
0 – 24E0.00 (Fail)

5.9 All Possible Grades & Examination Conditions

Conditions determining examination results based on Continuous Assessment (Assignment) and Semester Examination:

# Assignment (CA) Exam (SE) Result Official Meaning
1 Any Marks Marks < 40 I(SE) Exam marks below 40. Repeat examination required.
2 Any Marks Marks ≥ 40 A+ to C Graded according to the 5.10 Grading System.
3 Any Marks Absent with valid reason DFR Deferred (Approved medical / valid justification).
4 Any Marks Absent without valid reason AB Absent without justification (Counts as an attempt).
5 Not Submitted Marks ≥ 40 I(CA) Assignment not submitted. CA resubmission required.
6 Not Submitted Marks < 40 INC Incomplete: Assignment not submitted and Exam < 40.
7 Not Submitted Absent with valid reason I(CA) CA not submitted. Resubmission required.
8 Not Submitted Absent without valid reason AB Absent without valid reason.
